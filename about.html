About Us

Decor designed around your celebration

SweetdreamsbyMily creates personalized event décor with attention to theme, color, and the details that make each celebration unique. Our goal is to transform our clients' ideas into beautiful and memorable events while providing creative and reliable service.

The heart behind SweetdreamsbyMily

My name is Mily, and I create with love, compassion, and a grateful heart. As a cancer survivor, I cherish every single day of my life and the moments I get to share with the people I love. That appreciation for life inspires my wish to help others celebrate their own special moments.

I hold a bachelor’s degree in Business Administration, and I bring that knowledge into the planning, organization, and personal service behind SweetdreamsbyMily. I combine my creativity with a thoughtful approach to business to help make each celebration feel special and well cared for.

To me, a celebration is an opportunity to bring people together, share joy, and create a beautiful memory. Whether it is a birthday, a baby shower, a graduation, or another meaningful milestone, I want your event to feel personal and full of love.

Through SweetdreamsbyMily, I bring that care into the colors, decorations, and little details that reflect your story. My hope is to turn your celebration into a beautiful memory you and your loved ones can treasure for years to come.

Personal service from idea to setup

Share your inspiration, preferred colors, and event location. We help you choose décor that fits your celebration and coordinate setup and teardown details before your event.

From intimate gatherings to milestone celebrations, our designs bring together balloons, signs, backdrops, and finishing touches.
